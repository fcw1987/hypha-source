## Provenance and scope

The supplied v20 publication manifest reports an earlier audit of original Hypha feature histories, absence from the supplied Sites Vinext starter, repeated code segments, and copy or rename provenance. That historical review is supplied provenance; it was not independently reproduced in this publication checkout. The independent publication review verified the listed hashes against the v20 release snapshot, inspected the selected content and imports, compared it with available known starter or mixed files, and checked secrets and privacy. The full historical repository and starter template were not available locally.

The candidate `lib/rotation.ts` is omitted after a recognizable shared PRNG implementation was identified. The remaining 29 source and test files are unchanged from the reviewed release. The static guide and publication documents are handoff additions. Relative guide links work from a repository subdirectory.

This source-viewing selection omits the Sites starter scaffolding, mixed entry points and styles, authorization and storage implementation, deployment configuration, package manifests, dependencies and product icon assets. References to third-party packages are API references. Their implementations and assets remain outside this snapshot. Review for provenance does not constitute a legal opinion or a guarantee of exclusive ownership.
